BEHIND THE STORY

### History of our film

  Robyn Stewart: flight attendant for 55 years, a living bridge between aviation’s past and present. She began flying before the Boeing 747’s first commercial flight and stayed aloft through seismic industry shifts: airline deregulation, the industry’s glittering golden age and its long hangover, and the trauma of September 11th. She buried her husband Henry in Frankfurt, walked into rehab, walked back out, and returned to the job she loved.

Her son Caleb grew up in airport terminals and hotel lobbies, raised by a mother whose office was at 35,000 feet. What began as a nine-minute college film project became something much larger. Golden Wings is the documentary born from that evolution — a deeply human chronicle of family, career, and the stubborn instinct to keep going.

Assembled from decades of home video, rare archival footage, and candid new interviews with aviation insiders (including Jay Ricks, author of the original 747 training program, and Jock Bethune, a steward of industry lore) Golden Wings weaves personal memory with aviation history. This feature documentary highlights three generations linked by flight, exploring themes of resilience, loss, and the changing culture of air travel.

### Our Mission

  Our Mission — Honoring Flight Attendants, Preserving Aviation History

At Golden Wings, we celebrate the remarkable legacy of flight attendants—the unsung heroes of aviation history. Our documentary spotlights their resilience, professionalism, and unwavering dedication while documenting how they shaped passenger safety, in-flight service, and airline culture across decades.

What we do

- Preserve oral histories and archival materials that capture the experiences of flight attendants from the early jets to the modern cockpit.

- Showcase training, safety leadership, and crisis response skills that make flight attendants essential to every flight.

- Reveal the social and cultural impact flight attendants have had on gender roles, labor rights, and global travel.

Why it matters

- Flight attendants are critical to passenger safety and emergency response; their stories teach valuable lessons in risk management and human-centered care.

- Their careers reflect broader historical shifts—civil rights, women’s workplace advances, union movements, and changing standards in hospitality and security.

- Telling these stories preserves a vital piece of aviation heritage for researchers, enthusiasts, and future generations.
