  About Golden Wings

  A Personal Family Story with Universal ThemesGolden Wings transcends the typical documentary about a flight attendant; it embodies a rich tapestry of legacy, family bonds, and the spirit of resilience. Caleb’s grandfather, a World War II veteran, later emerged as a flight instructor for American Airlines, laying the foundation for a legacy that would soar through generations. Robyn, inspired by her father’s dedication, embraced her role in aviation, continuing a proud family tradition. This film serves not only as a tribute to Caleb’s family, but also as an anthem for everyone who dares to chase their dreams, no matter the obstacles.

Why This Story MattersThis documentary is a showcase of personal fortitude and historical significance. It delves into the realities faced by female flight attendants in the 1970s, grapples with the profound impact of 9/11, and explores the journey of overcoming alcohol dependency. Robyn’s path resonates with countless individuals, illustrating how monumental challenges can pave the way for remarkable victories. Whether you're passionate about aviation, inspired by tales of resilience, or captivated by the love that nourishes a family, Golden Wings delivers a universal narrative of hope and strength.
