# Golden Wings: An Award-Winning Documentary on Family Legacy and Aviation

Nov 14

Written By [Caleb Stewart](/indie-doc-journey?author=6629724930ab2f215b26ef6e)

![](/blog/7f26117eb3a4.png)

Robyn Stewart (the subjecy of Golden Wings) with her son, Caleb out the Lammle Monica Movie Theater during their screening at The Newsfest.

  Golden Wings is an award-winning documentary
It explores family legacy, resilience, and aviation history. After a recent screening at Newsfest, where the film won Best Trailer, Most Inspirational, and Most Outstanding, Golden Wings continues to resonate with audiences of all ages. This is a story about dedication and legacy, seen through the lens of one family’s journey.

![](/blog/7b63afa2232e.png)

Caleb and Robyn at the [Silicon Beach Film Festival](https://indiedocjourney.wordpress.com/2024/08/14/silicon-beach-film-festival-from-student-project-to-hollywood-venue/) where Golden Wings the documentary first screened the rough cut version at the historic Chinese Theater in Hollywood.

  About the Film

Golden Wings follows the story of Robyn Stewart, a flight attendant who dedicated 55 years of her life to aviation. The documentary explores not only her career but the resilience and family legacy she carries. Through her story, we glimpse the changing landscape of the airline industry and the sacrifices made by those who serve.

![](/blog/3e1aa8693ddc.jpg)

Robyn Stewart in a still from Golden Wings

  Audience Reaction & Festival Reception

At Newsfest, audiences connected deeply with Golden Wings. A woman around Robyn’s age shared how much the story meant to her as a mother, while a younger viewer admitted he’d never found aviation history interesting until he watched the film. These reactions highlight the film’s broad appeal, reaching viewers across generations.

  the Challenges of an Earlier Era

One of the notable scenes includes clips from the “Fly Me” airline commercials of the 1970s, which showcase the sexism flight attendants faced. Though meant as a serious reflection, these ads also bring out a mix of laughter and discomfort from audiences today, highlighting how much societal views have changed.

![](/blog/acc2340c762b.jpg)

Unsettling ads like this were what FA’s had to contend with

  Honoring Bette Nash’s Legacy

The documentary also pays tribute to Bette Nash, a senior flight attendant who continued to fly well into her eighties while caring for her son with special needs. Bette’s memorial appears at the end of the film, after a heartfelt letter from Robyn’s son, allowing audiences a moment of reflection on the dedication and resilience of those in aviation.

![](/blog/507f5969c4f6.png)

Bette Nash is mentioned by Robyn as a role model in the film

  Awards and Accolades

Golden Wings has already gained recognition in the festival circuit, sweeping all nominated categories at Newsfest. The film took home Best Trailer, Most Inspirational, and Most Outstanding awards, underscoring its emotional impact and storytelling strength.
