# From the galley: how this works

Former crew and their families can send photographs and stories through the [crew door on the home page](/#crew).

We read every submission. If we publish one here, it goes up under **From the Galley** with the contributor's name and airline, and only after we write to them first. Some stories may also be considered for the film — again, with a note to the person who shared it before anything is used.

This post is the how-it-works note, not a reader story. The reader stories come next, from you.
